# Surface Brief: Home & Explorer

Scope: Primary landing page & interactive mentorship explorer for first-time and returning developers.
Mode: Persuade transitioning to Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The open-source mentorship ecosystem is not a spreadsheet or a chaotic wall of cards; it is an illuminated transit system and launchpad guiding first-time contributors directly from their current skills to accepted, paid Linux Foundation mentorships. Refuses the unguided spreadsheet-dump and cookie-cutter SaaS hero.

OWN-WORLD: Deep midnight enamel base (#070b14, #0b1329), porcelain white typography (#f8fafc), laser-precise route lines (Cobalt #3b82f6, Emerald #10b981, Amber #f59e0b, Rose #f43f5e), sharp interchange badges, tactile filter tracks.

STORY: First-time visitor understands what LFX Mentorship is in 5 seconds (stipends, 80+ orgs, 560+ projects, active terms), gets guided on how to prepare and apply, clicks their preferred track (e.g. beginner tech, cloud native, systems), and immediately drives the full interactive explorer with search, saved bookmarks, and relationship graph.

FIRST VIEWPORT: High-impact transit launchpad hero with immediate stats ticker, 3-step newcomer roadmap pill, quick-start technology junctions (Go, Python, TypeScript, Rust), and instant split toggle ("Landing Guide" vs "Full Mission Control"), with primary search and action right at eye level.

FORM: Metropolitan rail map fired as midnight-blue enamel mural (wayfinding-cartography-signage-midnight-transit-diagram, seed e3ac80f1).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
